# Workflow — Counter Sale, Order Bundles and Delivery

For walk-in customers who buy products or bring their own cloth for stitching. No project is needed.

| # | Step | Who | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Find or add customer** | Counter staff | Billing → Counter Sale | Search by phone/email, or quick-add a Walk-in. New walk-ins are tagged *Walk-in*. |
| 2 | **Add product lines** | Counter staff | Counter Sale | Choose products, qty, rate, GST type. Option **deduct stock** with a warehouse. |
| 3 | **Optional installation** | Counter staff | Counter Sale | An installation charge creates a task; assign an employee or leave it open for anyone to pick. |
| 4 | **Optional stitching/making** | Counter staff | Line → needs work | A "Stitching Charges" line (5% GST) is added and an order **bundle** is created with a sticker code like `JB-0042`. |
| 5 | **Collect payment** | Counter staff | *Collect now* + method | Invoice is marked paid. Otherwise it stays Generated with a balance. |
| 6 | **Print stickers** | Counter staff | Invoice page | 50×25, 4×6 or A4 sticker plus job card. |
| 7 | **Process order** | Tailor / workers | Orders (scan sticker) | Status: ORDER → PROCESS → COMPLETED. Admin/Manager can skip or step back (logged). |
| 8 | **Install (if sold)** | Installer | Orders | Press *Mark Installed*. |
| 9 | **Deliver** | Employee | Goods Delivery (admin) or portal Requests → Goods Delivery | Collect the full pending balance; Process orders need a remark. Status becomes DELIVERED. |
| 10 | **Return (if needed)** | Admin | Billing → Product Return | Return items against the invoice; stock goes back and refund is processed. |

**Notes**
- Cancelling the invoice cancels its bundles.
- Bundle tracking is internal; customers get no tracker for bundles.
- Counter sales post to the customer ledger and the Cash Book like any invoice.

## Quick Q&A
**Q: Stock did not decrease.** *Deduct stock* was off or the line has no catalogue product.
**Q: How do I find an order quickly?** Orders → type or scan the code; or employee search by phone, bill number or order code.
